SWAP / THE INFRASTRUCTURE CHALLENGE

Keeping a growing marketplace free.

SWAP was designed to be free for everyone. Following unexpected growth, our Supabase account was terminated. Rather than introducing fees, I built a NAS server entirely from discarded and salvaged computer parts as an alternative infrastructure approach.

01 / PURPOSE

Free for everyone

No listing fees, subscriptions or charges for users.

02 / SETBACK

Sudden growth

Growing usage forced us to rethink the backend and hosting arrangement.

03 / RESPONSE

Scrap-built NAS

I repurposed salvaged hardware into a NAS to support a self-hosted approach without passing costs on.

THE LESSON: Engineering doesn't end at the codebase. Scaling requires infrastructure, resourcefulness and ownership of the systems behind a product.
